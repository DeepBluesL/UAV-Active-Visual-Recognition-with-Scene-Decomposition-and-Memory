# Predeclared active evidence experiment

Protocol fixed before development evaluation on 2026-10-23.

- Development: the existing 90 DEVELOPMENT scenes; selection: the 10 VALIDATION scenes (19/20 in each class block). No old 100-scene policy generates development supervision or enters the new models.
- After internal selection, retrain on all 100 TRAIN scenes. Seeds: 42–46. The frozen ResNet18 classifier, ImageNet ResNet50 features, and pretrained SAM decomposition are shared. Previously inspected simple/hard splits are exploratory benchmark re-evaluations, not untouched confirmation sets.
- Visual input: concatenate fixed 64-dimensional Gaussian projections of per-image standardized raw and SAM ResNet50 features, plus bbox/256 and position/100. Projection seed 1729 is fixed and not fitted. Scene IDs, filenames, true labels and unvisited observations are not model inputs.
- Gain-only model: 25 Bernoulli logits predict recognition correctness after each legal move or STOP. Full training-scene one-hop outcomes supply supervised labels. Inference uses only the current observation. Predicted move gain is predicted next correctness minus predicted STOP correctness.
- Gate: three Bernoulli heads estimate correctness of replace, retain and half-half probability fusion. Train on causal random/geometric walks with mixed accumulator updates, without validation/test labels. Hard argmax selects a candidate; ties prefer replace, then retain, then fuse. Zero-probability invisible observations and duplicate viewpoints cannot add evidence. An entirely invisible history remains Unknown.
- Joint model: freeze the trained gate, generate training prefixes with that gate, and supervise a separate gain model using correctness of the candidate gate-updated evidence. Its state contains the current observation and causal evidence accumulator. Future observations are used only to create training targets.
- Models: MLP 128/64 hidden units with ReLU. AdamW lr=0.001, weight_decay=0.001, batch 2048, 20 fixed epochs; no test-selected checkpoint. Gate histories: 128 starts per training scene, four random/geometric movements. No epoch/hyperparameter search after test evaluation.
- Movement: original 24 moves (10–40 m) plus STOP, maximum five moves, common hard distance caps 0/10/20/40/60 m, all 605 starting points. Unaffordable and boundary-invalid moves are masked before selection. All models may stop early; same cap does not imply equal realized distance.
- Cost coefficients: 0/0.0005/0.001/0.002 per metre. For each learned controller select one coefficient on internal validation, maximizing mean (accuracy − 0.001 × realized metres) over nonzero budgets, averaged over development seeds. Tie-break: smaller realized distance, then larger coefficient. Freeze selections before loading simple/hard data.
- References: stationary, sweep and random with confidence threshold 0.90; the historical matching-seed IBE-MAP final checkpoint is a separately labeled 100-scene reference at final evaluation only. It is adapted only by the same legal budget mask.
- On every controller's identical acquired paths compare final view, maximum-confidence observed view, unique-view probability mean and learned gate. Also compare gain-only/final with joint/gate (paths may differ).
- Primary outputs: accuracy versus hard cap and realized distance, paired gate-minus-final on exactly the same paths, rescue/harm, Unknown fraction, moves and gate-operation counts. Gain calibration: Brier scores and predicted-versus-realized one-step gain bins on internal validation; labels do not alter actions.
- Uncertainty: five new-module seeds, paired t intervals on fixed scenes; deterministic references counted once. Scene summaries retained. No universal novelty or statistically corrected superiority claim.
- Audit: input/cache/checkpoint hashes, fixed training/selection manifest, per-episode rollout arrays, independent boundary/distance/readout aggregate verification, and unit tests for causality, visibility and budgets. Publish bounded source and result summaries; dataset, model weights and full caches remain local.

This is one-step observation value prediction. It does not solve multi-step lookahead requiring an initially unhelpful move, calibrate the upstream classifier, fine-tune SAM, or remove the ground-truth-box assumption.
